About
Mental Math, Made Visual

Dr. UVC Math™ is the learning-and-enrichment side of Dr. UV's work. Where Dr. UVC Markets reads the markets across seven timeframes, Dr. UVC Math reads numbers the same way — looking for the pattern underneath.

Every method here — squaring near a base, the vinculum, the terrific two, pan-digital wonders — is taught the same way: animated, colored, and self-weaving, so the "why" is obvious before the "how" ever feels like work. Understanding first. Speed follows on its own.

The interactive lessons are free and always will be. The printable packs, primers, and the full Puzzle Composer are there when you want to take the wonder home — to the classroom, the kitchen table, or a curious kid.
