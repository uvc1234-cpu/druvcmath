The 25 primes below 100 — and the 25 riddles that hide in them

Colour by family: 2, 3, 5 and 7 take their own colours, and every larger prime follows the colour of its last digit. Every prime ends in 1, 3, 7 or 9 — which is Riddle 10's secret, sitting in plain sight.

Five levels, one skill

  1. Level 1 — Meet the Primes. The even one, the twins, the mirror, the last guard.
  2. Level 2 — Digit Detectives. Prime digits, palindromes, carousels.
  3. Level 3 — Crossover Numbers. Where primes meet squares, cubes, Fibonacci and the taxicab.
  4. Level 4 — Rare Breeds. Mersennes, double Mersennes, and Euler's prime factory.
  5. Level 5 — The Grand Finale. Splitting a century, catching a fake, and guarding the thousand.
